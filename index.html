Arun in a Nutshell

I am a data scientist who likes turning raw numbers into clear answers. I believe good data work is part analysis and part storytelling, and I try to do both in every project.

Most of my time goes into Python, SQL and machine learning: cleaning data, finding patterns, and building models that people can actually use. My skills include data analysis, visualization and machine learning, and I enjoy end-to-end projects that start with collecting data and end with a working app or dashboard.

Outside work I build personal projects such as a stock tracking dashboard and a trading bot, and I write about what I learn on Medium and Towards Data Science. Browse my projects and articles below, and feel free to open the code and try it yourself.
